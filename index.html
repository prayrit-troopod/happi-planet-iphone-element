FAQs
Does it have a strong smell?

No, it has a pleasant, mild fragrance that doesn't leave any lasting chemical odor.

How often should I use this product?

Use it daily to keep a clean and fresh bathroom. For areas with hard water buildup, you may use it 2-3 times a week initially until buildup is removed.

How does the foaming action work?

The unique foaming formula clings to vertical surfaces, allowing maximum contact time with limescale and stains. This ensures better cleaning efficiency compared to regular liquid cleaners that run off quickly.

What types of surfaces can I use this cleaner on?

It's specially formulated to be gentle yet effective on all bathroom and kitchen fixtures, including taps, faucets, mirrors, sinks, glass, tiles, sink, fittings, shower heads, etc.
